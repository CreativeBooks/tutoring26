Weekly Breakdown:

Week 1: Functions and Inverses (Unit 1)
Focus: Master the overarching behavior of parent functions and the algebraic/graphical mechanics of finding inverses.

Week 2: Logarithms and Rate of Change (Unit 2)
Focus: Bridge exponential relationships to logarithmic operations and explore transcendental applications.

Week 3: Polynomial & Rational Functions (Units 3 & 6)
Focus: Synthesize higher-degree equations and expressions, relying heavily on advanced factoring techniques to analyze discontinuities.

Week 4: Triangles, Surface Area, Volume, & Geometric Modeling (Unit 4) Focus: Review logical geometric reasoning and transition into three-dimensional coordinate and spatial transformations.

Week 5: Circle Geometry (Unit 5)
Focus: Investigate planar circle theorems and derive algebraic representations of conic circles.

Week 6: The Unit Circle, Trigonometry, & Statistics (Units 7 & 8)
Focus: Transition trigonometric functions from right triangles to periodic wave tracking, concluding with data analytics foundations.
